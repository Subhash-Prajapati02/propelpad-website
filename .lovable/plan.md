# Propelpad — premium white store website

## Client and audience
Propelpad provides food-commerce fulfilment for frozen and specialty food brands through nine white stores in Mumbai and Thane. The website should speak to brand founders and operations teams, not consumers ordering food.

## Design
An editorial, bright-white and electric-blue identity inspired by the presentation, with charcoal typography, oversized confident headings and precise operational details. Use Manrope typography and monospace labels. A full-bleed cinematic facility-led opening contrasts with generous white content bands. Glass overlays are functional, restrained and readable.

## Content and interactions
- Brand-led opening with verified network facts and partnership CTA.
- Authentic partner logos extracted from the presentation.
- White store standards, dark/white comparison, and measured operational performance.
- Sticky cinematic facility sequence using actual presentation photos, with storage, handling and dispatch standards.
- Interactive SKU and buying intelligence sections.
- Selectable Mumbai/Thane locations and clearly labelled FY26–27 planned expansion.
- Contact section with verified email and phone links; inquiry composer opens an email draft rather than pretending to send.

## Technical details
Build at the index route with section anchors. Extract and optimize original PPTX media and serve it through Lovable Assets. Use lightweight intersection observers and requestAnimationFrame scroll effects, with reduced-motion fallbacks. Keep business data centralized and add concrete fact tests. Verify real browser behavior at desktop, tablet and mobile sizes. No invented claims, testimonials, live backend or fabricated facility imagery.
